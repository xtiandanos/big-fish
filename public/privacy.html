Privacy Policy — Big Fish

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Overview

Big Fish is a free browser game. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data on our own servers. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking cookies of our own.

Facebook Login & Sharing

The game offers optional Facebook features (login and score sharing) powered by the Facebook SDK provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you choose to log in with Facebook, Facebook may process information in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy. The game only uses your Facebook login status to decide whether to show the share button — it never reads your friends list, posts, or other profile data, and it does not store your Facebook information.

Third-party services

The game is hosted on Vercel, which serves the static files of the game. See Vercel's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle standard web request data.

Contact

Questions? Reach out via the game's GitHub repository issues page.
